# Mission — sec-12-methodology-and-reporting

**Started**: not yet · **Family**: sec · **Phase**: S2 · **Milestone**: not yet allocated

## Why

Sam wants to test his own systems seriously — the router edition especially, which the planning
conversation flagged as a real security responsibility. Serious testing needs a method: a lawful scope, honest evidence, a
defensible severity, and a report a reader can act on. This topic is the discipline that makes the
S2 techniques trustworthy and carries into S3, where the target is Sam's own Syntek OS. It is also
what separates authorised testing from the kind the law forbids.

_Drafted from Sam's planning conversation of 27/09/2026 — confirm or rewrite in your own words at the first lesson._

## Can do it when

- Sam can name the phases of NIST SP 800-115, PTES and the OWASP WSTG and pick the right one for an engagement.
- Sam can write a rules-of-engagement and scope document that makes a lab test lawful and unambiguous.
- Sam can keep contemporaneous, reproducible evidence for each finding.
- Sam can score a finding with a CVSS v4.0 base vector and explain what it does and does not mean.
- Sam can write a report whose findings are reproducible and carry a concrete remediation.
